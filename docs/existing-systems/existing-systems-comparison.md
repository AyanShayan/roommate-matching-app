## 1. Introduction

Finding a suitable roommate is an important challenge for university students, especially when sharing dormitories or rented apartments.

Several existing platforms help people search for rooms and potential roommates. However, these services differ in their approaches to lifestyle preferences, matching algorithms, communication, and security.

The purpose of this comparison is to examine existing roommate-finding solutions, identify their strengths and limitations, and explain how our proposed mobile application could address specific student needs.

Three existing systems were selected:

1. SpareRoom
2. Diggz
3. Roomi

The platforms are compared using ten consistent criteria.

## 2. Overview of Existing Systems

### 2.1. SpareRoom

**Website:** https://www.spareroom.com/

SpareRoom is an accommodation-sharing platform that helps users find available rooms and potential roommates.

Users can search for accommodation, apply filters, browse advertisements, and communicate with other users.

The platform provides mobile applications for Android and iOS.

**Strengths:**
- Search by location and budget.
- Room and roommate advertisements.
- Search filters.
- Integrated communication.
- Mobile applications.
- Listing moderation.

**Limitations:**
- The main workflow focuses on accommodation listings.
- A dedicated student lifestyle questionnaire is not established in the reviewed documentation.
- An individual ML-based compatibility percentage was not confirmed.
- Some contact features require upgraded access.

### 2.2. Diggz

**Website:** https://www.diggz.co/

Diggz is a roommate-finding service that focuses on matching people based on their living habits and preferences.

The platform describes a proprietary algorithm that considers factors such as lifestyle, budget, location, and personal preferences.

Users can browse profiles, express interest, and communicate with potential roommates.

**Strengths:**
- Personalized roommate matching.
- Lifestyle compatibility preferences.
- Budget and location filters.
- Mutual interest functionality.
- Profile verification options.
- Integrated messaging.

**Limitations:**
- The platform is not exclusively designed for university students.
- The details of its proprietary matching algorithm are not publicly established.
- Detailed explanations of individual compatibility predictions were not confirmed.
- Dedicated university student verification was not confirmed.

### 2.3. Roomi

**Website:** https://roomiapp.com/

Roomi is an online service designed to help people find roommates and shared accommodation.

The platform provides profiles, search filters, communication features, and identity verification options.

Public product information also describes roommate matching based on user preferences.

**Strengths:**
- Roommate and accommodation search.
- Profile-based filtering.
- Identity verification options.
- Safety features.
- Integrated messaging.
- Mobile application support.

**Limitations:**
- The platform targets general roommate seekers.
- Some communication and verification features may depend on selected plans.
- Publicly available documentation does not establish a supervised ML model with published evaluation results.
- Detailed explanations of compatibility predictions were not confirmed.

## 5. Proposed Application

Our project proposes a mobile application designed specifically for university students.

The application will use Machine Learning to analyze lifestyle preferences and recommend compatible roommates.

### Planned Core Features

- Student registration and authentication.
- Student profile management.
- Lifestyle compatibility questionnaire.
- ML-based roommate recommendations.
- Compatibility scores and explanations.
- Preference and accommodation filters.
- Viewing potential roommate profiles.
- Privacy and security controls.

Additional features, such as messaging and student-status verification, may be included depending on development priorities.

## 7. Discussion

The comparison shows that existing roommate-finding platforms already offer useful features such as searching, messaging, profile creation, and personalized matching.

SpareRoom focuses strongly on accommodation search and communication.

Diggz provides compatibility-oriented roommate matching based on lifestyle preferences.

Roomi combines roommate search with filtering, communication, and verification options.

Our project does not claim to be the first roommate-matching application or the first service to use compatibility algorithms.

Instead, we aim to develop a student-focused mobile application with a machine learning model that can be evaluated against a rule-based baseline.

Our approach will also investigate explainable recommendations, reciprocal preferences, and privacy-conscious student profiles.

## 8. Connection to Student Interviews

Five student interviews were conducted on October 18, 2026.

The interviews identified important concerns related to:

- Sleep schedules and noise.
- Cleanliness and household responsibilities.
- Personal boundaries.
- Financial responsibility.
- Guest preferences.
- Trust and profile verification.
- Communication before choosing a roommate.

These findings support the planned lifestyle questionnaire and student-oriented application design.

However, the small interview sample is exploratory and cannot represent the entire university student population.

## 9. Identified Opportunities

The proposed project will focus on the following opportunities:

**Opportunity 1: University Student Focus**

Develop a roommate-finding application specifically designed around student accommodation needs.

**Opportunity 2: Structured Compatibility Questionnaire**

Collect lifestyle information relevant to roommate compatibility.

**Opportunity 3: ML-Based Recommendations**

Train and evaluate a supervised machine learning model using appropriately collected compatibility data.

**Opportunity 4: Explainable Matching Results**

Provide understandable information about why students may be compatible.

**Opportunity 5: Reciprocal Matching**

Consider the preferences and mandatory restrictions of both potential roommates.

**Opportunity 6: Privacy and Trust**

Minimize unnecessary collection and disclosure of personal information.

## 10. Conclusion

This study compared three existing roommate-finding platforms: SpareRoom, Diggz, and Roomi.

The comparison used ten criteria covering target users, matching functionality, communication, security, mobile availability, and geographic focus.

The analysis shows that existing platforms offer useful roommate-search features, but our project will investigate a more specific approach focused on university students.

The proposed application will combine lifestyle questionnaires, supervised machine learning, preference filtering, and explainable compatibility recommendations.

The final effectiveness of this approach will be determined through model evaluation, usability testing, and comparison with a baseline method.
