# Student Interview Guide

## Project Information

**Project:** Development of a Compatibility-Based Roommate Matching Application for University Students

**Research Purpose:** To identify problems students experience when finding roommates and investigate their preferences and expectations for an ML-based roommate matching mobile application.

**Target Participants:** University students with experience living with roommates or searching for shared accommodation.

**Number of Participants:** 3–5 students.

**Interview Method:** Semi-structured interviews.

**Estimated Duration:** 5–10 minutes per participant.

## Interview Questions

**Q1.** Have you ever lived with a roommate or searched for one?

**Q2.** How did you find your roommate, or how would you search for one?

**Q3.** What difficulties have you experienced or would you expect when choosing a roommate?

**Q4.** Which factors are most important to you when choosing a roommate? (For example: cleanliness, sleep schedule, noise tolerance, study habits, or budget.)

**Q5.** Have differences in daily habits ever caused conflicts with your roommate? If yes, what happened?

**Q6.** How do you currently evaluate whether someone would be a suitable roommate?

**Q7.** Would you consider using a mobile application that recommends roommates based on lifestyle compatibility? Why or why not?

**Q8.** Would you be comfortable completing a lifestyle questionnaire to receive personalized roommate recommendations?

**Q9.** How useful would a machine learning-based compatibility score be when choosing a roommate? What information would you need to trust it?

**Q10.** What features would you expect from a roommate matching mobile application?

## Interview Procedure

1. Explain the purpose of the project.
2. Ask for voluntary participation and permission to take anonymized notes.
3. Conduct the interview without encouraging particular answers.
4. Record the participant's responses accurately.
5. Identify common problems, preferences, and feature requests.
6. Summarize findings and their implications for system requirements.

## Privacy and Ethics

- Participation is voluntary.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal information.
- Use anonymous participant identifiers such as P1, P2, and P3.
- Do not publish participant names, contact details, or recordings.
- Clearly distinguish actual interview findings from project assumptions.

## Expected Deliverables

- Completed interviews with 3–5 students.
- Anonymized interview response summaries.
- A table of common findings and user needs.
- Requirements derived from interview findings.
- Documentation uploaded to GitHub for review.
